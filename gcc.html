Questions Evaluators Ask

How fast can a pod go live?

A pod is planned to be running in six to eight weeks. Smaller pods sit at the six week end; larger or two-campus pods take closer to eight.

Which pod model should we start with?

Innovation pods build new products and AI capability, Operational pods run applications, infrastructure and finance operations, and Hybrid pods do both in one team. Most clients start Hybrid.

Where is the team based?

In Hyderabad, Visakhapatnam, or across both campuses, led from our headquarters in Plano, Texas.

How is our work kept separate from other clients?

Each pod has its own room, its own network segment and its own support contacts, under ISO 27001 and SSAE 18 controls.

Can we start smaller than a full center?

Yes. Start with a statement of work, add on-demand talent, or stand up a dedicated center when the work is steady.
